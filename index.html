Transcription Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Transcription helps you save, copy, download, and organize captions that YouTube makes available for a video you choose.

Information processed

When you press the Transcript button, the extension reads the selected video's title, channel name, URL, caption language, and caption text from YouTube's current page and caption player requests. It processes that information to format and save the transcript. It does not read your clipboard.

The extension stores transcript copies in Chrome's local extension storage. If you have installed the optional Transcript Vault Mac app, the extension can also send a copy through Chrome Native Messaging to that app, which stores it in a local folder on your Mac. Notes, stars, and Trash state are stored locally by the Mac app. Capturing a transcript copies its text to the clipboard and downloads a text file by default. You can turn off either automatic action in the extension settings. Reader buttons also let you copy or download a saved transcript.

Where information goes

Transcript requests are made to YouTube as part of the video player and its caption service. Transcription has no Transcription account, analytics, advertising, remote transcript server, or cloud sync. Transcript data is not sent to the developer. The optional Mac app connection runs between Chrome and an app on the same computer.

Chrome's own sync, backup, or download features may handle data according to your Chrome settings. Transcription does not control those services.

Your choices and control

Use the extension settings to turn off automatic clipboard copying or downloads. Chrome copies can be removed by clearing the extension’s local data or uninstalling it. The Mac app has a recoverable Trash. Uninstalling the extension removes its Chrome local storage according to Chrome's behavior; uninstalling the Mac app does not automatically delete transcript files already saved on your Mac. You can delete those files from ~/Library/Application Support/Transcript Vault/. Downloaded text files remain in the folder where Chrome saved them after clearing extension data or uninstalling; delete those files separately.

Limited use

Transcription's use of information received from Google services adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Transcript data is used only to provide the transcript capture, storage, copy, download, and library features you request. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties by the developer.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the product's data practices change. The current version will be linked from the Chrome Web Store listing. Contact: Transcription support. Please do not post private transcript content in a public support issue.
